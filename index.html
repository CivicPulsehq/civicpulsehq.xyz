What is CivicPulse?

CivicPulse is an infrastructure and urban intelligence initiative exploring how engineering, geospatial technologies, AI, and data analytics help create smarter, more sustainable, and resilient cities. It brings together research, digital experimentation, and knowledge-sharing through a connected ecosystem.
